# Top 50 learnings from the donor audit, ranked (2026-10-12)

The 50 most useful rules we learned from 100+ donor games, ranked by value to a playable, fun, safe, sellable Frankendom.
Part 1 is the PHONE VERSION (copy it straight out). Part 2 is the full version with donor games and tags.
Draft for Dom and Strategy. Not committed. Standing rules respected: no death penalty, never gear loss, barter-only player trade, no kill-N or collect-N quests, take-one loot, favour is a long-lived ledger, parties and guilds deferred.

---

# PART 1: PHONE VERSION

## Do now (1-10)
1. The server replays every fight before anything is paid.
   Cheating cannot pay, so every reward means something.
2. Every item or metal move is checked in full, then done in one step.
   No lost items and no duplicated loot.
3. Each zone refills its own creatures, one at a time, out of sight.
   Zones feel alive and nothing pops up in your face.
4. Zones nobody is in go to sleep; far creatures get checked rarely.
   700 zones cost almost nothing until players arrive.
5. If the server falls behind, it drops the backlog instead of bursting.
   No sudden stalls or speed-ups for everyone.
6. "Which zone am I in" comes from the zone rows, not a short fixed list.
   Every one of the 700 zones can actually run.
7. Loot is worked out first: what fits goes in, the rest stays claimable.
   A full pack never eats your take-one pick.
8. A matching stack tops up before it needs a new slot.
   Fewer "pack full" refusals.
9. Actions are limited per account, and a refusal changes nothing.
   Phone users on shared networks are not punished for others.
10. A quest reward pays once, only if the bag has room, all or nothing.
   Finishing a quest always feels clean.

## Next (11-20)
11. A hunting creature has moods: notice, chase, search, go home.
   Enemies chase you, give up, and reset instead of standing still.
12. Pursuit ends on a clock, and weak creatures run from a much stronger player.
   Fights end fairly instead of dragging across the map.
13. A creature sticks with its target unless another is clearly ahead.
   Enemies stop flickering between heroes.
14. Only a few enemies attack at once; the rest wait their turn.
   Crowds stay fair and readable on a small screen.
15. The thumb stick ramps up smoothly, and a tap during recovery is not lost.
   Controls feel right on the phone.
16. One budget for effects, one picture-cost ceiling, one quality ladder.
   The game stays smooth on weaker phones.
17. Shops quote one price, check room first, and buy back for less than they sell.
   A clean first place to spend metal.
18. Swapping gear is one tap and puts the old piece back in the pack.
   No fiddly unequip steps.
19. Every zone move uses one path and lands you outside the trigger.
   Zones connect without stuck or exploitable doors.
20. Loot tables pay by rules, and weak or repeat victims pay less.
   Farming the easy stuff stops being the best plan.

## Soon (21-30)
21. Teach one hint at a time, when it matters, and always allow skip.
   New players are guided, never lectured.
22. Always show what is next, and open town services one at a time.
   Players know their next goal after every fight.
23. An enemy health bar shows only when targeted, hurt or hunting you.
   A clean screen with the useful bars.
24. The map fits the zone, shows a direction line, and reveals only what you know.
   You find the forge without getting lost.
25. The camera sits over the shoulder and pulls in before walls.
   You can always see your hero.
26. Many hits on one target show as one clear reaction.
   Group fights stay readable.
27. Bosses show their next move, change at health steps, and cap helpers at three.
   Boss fights are tough but fair.
28. Elite and boss versions look like the base creature with clear extra cues.
   You read the danger at a glance.
29. Zones get names, signs, a short card and rumours from a seeded set.
   Each zone feels like a place.
30. Your own walking shows at once, and the server quietly corrects drift.
   Moving feels instant even on a bad signal.

## Later (31-40)
31. Walking speed is limited by real elapsed time, warn first, then act.
   Speed hacks fail without punishing a laggy phone.
32. Each fight record covers every random draw and names the first bad moment.
   Disputes and bugs get solved quickly.
33. Suspicious accounts are flagged for a person, never banned by a machine.
   Honest players are not banned by mistake.
34. Chat has one gate: refused lines cost nothing, then a warning, then a timed mute.
   Chat stays calm from day one.
35. Who can hear whom is one gate: staff, block, privacy, friends only.
   Blocked really means blocked.
36. A report carries the last lines you saw; a person picks the mute length.
   One report never silences anyone for good.
37. Barter trade closes if players move apart, and any change resets both confirms.
   Safe swaps with no scams and no lost items.
38. The server sends only what is near and what changed; slow phones get fewer updates.
   One slow phone cannot hurt the rest.
39. Each camp has its own seed, and each system its own random stream.
   One camp can be rebuilt alone and fights replay exactly.
40. Every asset has a licence row, and a new asset without one fails the check.
   We can sell the game without licence surprises.

## Parked (41-50)
41. Content is checked when it loads, with named reasons for any refusal.
   Bad zone data never reaches players.
42. A zone row can name a parent and change only a few keys.
   700 zones are easier to author.
43. Repeated stuns weaken, then the target is immune for a while.
   No stun-lock in boss or group fights.
44. In group fights, credit follows who really did the damage.
   Loot goes to people who earned it.
45. One world clock drives day, night and townsfolk daily routines.
   Towns look lived-in.
46. World events fire once, with the end time sent by the server.
   Banners and countdowns always agree.
47. A hit can pull the camp, with a cap; some camps ambush or patrol.
   Camps feel like groups, not statues.
48. Zone runs have a hard lifetime and a grace when empty.
   Staged zone runs clean up after themselves.
49. One slot per status effect on a target; the stronger replaces the weaker.
   Status effects stay readable.
50. Ranked duels pair players in a rating window that widens with waiting.
   Fair, fast matches later.

# PART 2: FULL VERSION (donors and tags)

2. A learning is a rule that transfers, not a task. It rests on what the audit found in donor games.
3. The 51 gap-check files said what Frankendom already has. Many learnings are "keep doing this".
4. Tiers: 1 Build first, 2 Core loop, 3 Fun and feel, 4 Safety and scale, 5 Later.
5. Tags: layer is Engine, Core or Modes. This is my reading of the 10-10 layer ruling, not quoted. Treat as a guess.
6. "Phone-first yes" means it works at 375 wide with no new cost. "no" means a risk to watch.
7. Donors under GPL, AGPL or a restricted licence give ideas only. I carry no donor numbers.
8. Every number must come from our own tests and specs.
9. Withdrawn rulings are not used. Death penalty, item loss, reclaim delay, skill-ups by use, money in player trades: all out.
10. Where checkers found a note wrong, I used the corrected version or dropped it.
11. Part 1 above is the same list in two lines each, with no donors.
12. Stop after the top 10 if you only want the shape.

## TOP 10
1. Server judges, the phone asks.
2. One ledger, all or nothing.
3. A zone that runs.
4. Sleep what nobody is near.
5. The server clock never bursts.
6. Zone lookup comes from data.
7. Take-one loot is planned before it is written.
8. Stack-first placement.
9. Limits per account.
10. A quest reward pays once or not at all.

## Tier 1: Build first

1. **Server judges, the phone asks.** The phone sends what it did, never the result; the server replays from the seed and inputs before it pays.
   Apply: every new reward path (loot, metal, standing, quest) pays only after the server replay agrees.
   Seen in: OpenRA, Hedgewars, Showdown. [Engine] [phone-first yes]

2. **One ledger, all or nothing.** Check every owner, count, balance and room first, then move everything in one step, one named reason per flow.
   Apply: items and metal use the one ledger; each source and drain of metal gets a closed reason so Stats can total them.
   Seen in: SpacetimeDB, LostCityRS, OpenRA. [Core] [phone-first yes]

3. **A zone that runs.** One director per zone owns timers, at most one creature appears per fire, only out of sight, and a live cap counts only what is alive.
   Apply: a spawn and respawn director that reads the zone row, with a jittered delay.
   Seen in: Shattered Pixel Dungeon, Daggerfall Unity, DOOM. [Core] [phone-first yes]

4. **Sleep what nobody is near.** Empty zones freeze (not delete) after a grace; creatures near players are checked often, far ones rarely.
   Apply: a work budget per tick and a freeze rule so 700 zones are cheap.
   Seen in: EQEmu, dhewm3, OpenGothic. [Core] [phone-first yes]

5. **The server clock never bursts.** Cap catch-up steps and drop the backlog; never fire a burst of missed work.
   Apply: add the cap to the presence tick before any server creature work runs on it.
   Seen in: colyseus, SuperTuxKart, ioquake3. [Engine] [phone-first yes]

6. **Zone lookup comes from data.** One registry built from zone rows; overlaps refused at load; outside any zone means nowhere.
   Apply: replace the two hard-coded zones so all 700+ can run.
   Seen in: L2Solo, Microverse, LostCityRS. [Core] [phone-first yes]

7. **Take-one loot is planned before it is written.** Work out requested, placed and leftover first; take-one is all or nothing and stays claimable if the pack is full; leftovers never go on the floor.
   Apply: one loot intake plan used by every drop.
   Seen in: Angband, Forgotten Server, Endless Sky. [Core] [phone-first yes]

8. **Stack-first placement.** A matching stack tops up before a free slot is needed; the remainder stays at the source.
   Apply: stackable pickups no longer fail on a full pack.
   Seen in: Shattered Pixel Dungeon, EQEmu, Barony. [Core] [phone-first yes]

9. **Limits per account.** Count actions per account and per kind of action; a refusal returns a clear reason and changes nothing.
   Apply: needed before many new item actions open; phones share carrier addresses, so address limits are not enough.
   Seen in: OpenRCT2, SpacetimeDB, Nakama. [Engine] [phone-first yes]

10. **A quest reward pays once or not at all.** Check bag room before any write; one claim, once-only, all or nothing; the claim stays open until there is room.
   Apply: finish loot and favour rewards so they stop being refused. Objectives use guardian, event and travel steps, never kill-N or collect-N.
   Seen in: ModernUO, DOLSharp, EQEmu. [Core] [phone-first yes]

## Tier 2: Core loop

11. **A hunting creature has moods.** Notice, chase, search, go home, with a leash; clocks count server steps; it cannot be targeted while returning and refills on arrival.
   Apply: build on the existing creature modes, behind a preview flag. No behaviour-tree engine.
   Seen in: Crawl, Brogue, Veloren. [Core] [phone-first yes]

12. **Pursuit ends on a clock, not on a fence.** The chase memory is set by how clever the creature is; a score race of "keep chasing" against "let go" beats a fixed radius; a creature that flees stays fleeing until well recovered.
   Apply: memory time, flee gap and frozen turns are Dom's values, tuned in play.
   Seen in: Crawl, Veloren, Angband. [Core] [phone-first yes]

13. **A creature sticks with its target.** It switches only when a rival is clearly ahead, and drops an unreachable target after a short wait.
   Apply: add to the one-at-a-time attack token; no numbers copied.
   Seen in: AzerothCore, cMaNGOS, TrinityCore. [Modes] [phone-first yes]

14. **Only a few attack at once.** The rest queue for a turn.
   Apply: keep and enforce the small attacker cap in code.
   Seen in: KeeperFX, OpenDUNE, Angband. [Core] [phone-first yes]

15. **The stick ramps from zero.** Rescale the dead zone, reach full speed before full throw, and count a press so a tap in recovery is not lost.
   Apply: the phone feel fix; test two taps in one step first.
   Seen in: SRB2, Barony, Teeworlds. [Engine] [phone-first yes]

16. **One phone budget.** Add up every effect pool; set a draw-call and triangle ceiling per quality tier; one quality table; a ladder that drops fast, recovers slow and never touches warning cues.
   Apply: run on CI or the VPS; add a lower frame cap when the tab is not focused.
   Seen in: Mindustry, Luanti, Levy Street. [Engine] [phone-first yes]

17. **A shop quotes one price.** The server recomputes it, checks room before charge, moves item and metal in one step, and buys back for less than it sells; price can follow stock.
   Apply: add the vendor service; buy-back stays within Dom's cap.
   Seen in: 2004Scape, EQEmu, Veloren. [Core] [phone-first yes]

18. **Swap gear in one step.** The displaced piece returns to the pack; a table says which pairs exclude each other; one undo.
   Apply: replace the "slot is occupied" refusal.
   Seen in: Daggerfall Unity, Evennia, OpenTESArena. [Core] [phone-first yes]

19. **One path for every zone move.** Snapshot the mover once, land outside the trigger, no logout escape, and a data test that every link is answered.
   Apply: includes the door channel (starts in range, cancelled by moving away, fires once).
   Seen in: Card Forge, Quake, Veloren. [Core] [phone-first yes]

20. **Loot tables pay by rules; weak and repeat victims pay less.** Gate, weighted pick, minimum, a cap on rare drops per kill, and a grey band that pays nothing.
   Apply: mostly built; add the rare cap and the global pool.
   Seen in: EQEmu, Cubyz, KeeperRL. [Core] [phone-first yes]

## Tier 3: Fun and feel

21. **One hint at a time.** Shown when its state is reached, finished by a real action, once per account, with Skip always there.
   Apply: after fight 1, for later features.
   Seen in: Mindustry, Cataclysm, Endless Sky. [Core] [phone-first yes]

22. **Always show what is next.** A goal line ("N more") and services that open one at a time, flash until visited, and say why a door is locked.
   Apply: next opponent name is already in the display; add the service tour.
   Seen in: Barony, KeeperFX, 2004Scape. [Core] [phone-first yes]

23. **Enemy bars appear only when useful.** Shown when targeted, hurt or hunting you; hidden at full health; red when it targets you; first tap selects, second engages.
   Apply: the written rule is not built yet.
   Seen in: Veloren, KeeperRL, Angband. [Engine] [phone-first no, test at 375]

24. **A map that fits the zone.** One uniform fit, cached base, repaint only changed cells, server-filtered reveal, tap to centre, "forge, 12 m, north-east" line.
   Apply: no map exists today.
   Seen in: Mindustry, OpenRA, isometric-city. [Engine] [phone-first no, repaint cost]

25. **Over-the-shoulder camera with wall pull-in.** Fast in, slow out, fade a wall that is very close, keep the hero in frame.
   Apply: preview flag; the Pit camera must stay identical; Dom's open camera questions come first.
   Seen in: OpenMW, SRB2, OpenAC. [Engine] [phone-first no, hero in frame at 375]

26. **Many hits on one target read as one.** One merged reaction per victim per beat; a short immunity after a stun; floating numbers capped.
   Apply: needed once more than one attacker exists.
   Seen in: Quake, Veloren, Barony. [Engine] [phone-first no, clutter risk]

27. **Bosses show what they do next.** A visible pre-swing cue, move changes at health steps, helpers at a visible spot capped at three, an optional skippable walk-in.
   Apply: extend the existing boss machine.
   Seen in: DOOM, Veloren, DevilutionX. [Modes] [phone-first yes]

28. **Elite and boss versions are the same body, readable.** Two multipliers, a wider look pool, an element overlay with a non-colour cue, one swapped ability with a cue.
   Apply: row fields; check the 375 silhouette.
   Seen in: TrinityCore, flare-game, ModernUO. [Core] [phone-first no, silhouette]

29. **Zones have a voice.** Seeded name pools, a two-line zone card, signs by category, rumours that point at a real nearby entrance. No free text from players.
   Apply: all words are ours; legends stay public-domain.
   Seen in: Cataclysm, Daggerfall Unity, OpenTESArena. [Core] [phone-first yes]

30. **Your own walking shows at once.** The phone predicts, the server corrects, with a rollback timer.
   Apply: for the world walker; confirm it is not already built.
   Seen in: OTClient, colyseus. [Engine] [phone-first yes]

## Tier 4: Safety and scale

31. **Speed is limited by real elapsed time.** Credit never exceeds the clock; log first, then hard action; strikes survive a reconnect.
   Apply: write a test first; the spam exploit is suspected, not proven.
   Seen in: EQEmu, Luanti, claudecraft. [Engine] [phone-first yes]

32. **A fight's fingerprint covers every random draw.** A replay or verifier failure names the first bad checkpoint; a desync marks the connection and records the moment.
   Apply: widen the fingerprint; needs a version bump per the sim-change rule.
   Seen in: OpenRA, OpenBW, Beyond All Reason. [Engine] [phone-first yes]

33. **Flag, never auto-ban.** A sticky mark refuses awards; a person reviews a queue.
   Apply: account flag ledger.
   Seen in: Angband, lila, Warzone 2100. [Core] [phone-first yes]

34. **Chat has one intake gate.** Refused lines cost no budget; warn band, then timed mute; staff exempt; a flood closes the socket and costs a cool-off.
   Apply: build with chat, not after. Mute lengths are ruled.
   Seen in: Evennia, OpenDAoC, Veloren. [Core] [phone-first no, keyboard at 375]

35. **Who hears whom is one gate.** Fixed order: staff, block, privacy, friends only; unknown mode fails closed.
   Apply: same error for blocked and unknown.
   Seen in: LostCityRS, Nakama, ACE. [Core] [phone-first yes]

36. **A report carries evidence.** Copy the reporter's last server-stamped lines; count distinct reporters; a person picks the mute length.
   Apply: one report never means a permanent mute.
   Seen in: ModernUO, AAEmu, rAthena. [Core] [phone-first yes]

37. **Barter trade is safe by shape.** Both accept; a trade closes at once if players move apart; any edit resets both confirmations; offered items are held aside and return on cancel or disconnect; settle is all or nothing with a room check. Items only, no coin.
   Apply: wire it as a server module.
   Seen in: l1j, AzerothCore, EvEmu. [Core] [phone-first no, trade window at 375]

38. **Send only what is near and what changed.** Shed updates to slow phones instead of stalling the room.
   Apply: also note the writer's per-call process is the first thing to break at scale.
   Seen in: ioquake3, Teeworlds, SpacetimeDB. [Engine] [phone-first no, slow sockets]

39. **Seed each camp; give each system its own random stream.** One camp can be rebuilt alone; a new draw never shifts others.
   Apply: needed by the director and the verifier.
   Seen in: Cubyz, OpenBW, boardgame.io. [Core] [phone-first yes]

40. **Every asset has a licence row.** A test fails when a new asset has none; one credits page.
   Apply: TRELLIS.2 output is look-development only until its licence issue is settled.
   Seen in: our own asset audit (no single donor). [Engine] [phone-first yes]

## Learnings we deliberately SKIP and why
- Death costs (item loss, XP tax, reclaim waits, killer takes an item): standing rule is no death penalty and never gear loss.
- Skill gain by use and hit-chance dice: standing rule; fights are timing and contact.
- Money in player trades, auctions and fee tricks: trade is barter only.
- Kill-N and collect-N quests, and the running kill-count line: banned in Region 1.
- Behaviour-tree or planner engines, server rewind, lockstep waits: the duel brain is the brain and no live server fight exists. Music is parked.

## What we still do not know
- Pathfinding internals: no note says how a creature walks around an obstacle, and no collision data exists in the world files.
- Group tactics beyond the attack token (flanking, formations, focus fire), and replay formats of closed engines.
- About 130 older new-topic notes (sound 47, skills 20, magic 14, boss staging 7, procgen 6) were never checked; I did not read the wave-4 notes either, so sound and spells are left out of the 50.
- Things the gap-checks could not verify: whether the scripted first loss is live, whether the attacker-cap and crowd-control pieces are on trunk, where held loot leftovers live, whether the speed exploit is real, and the tuning numbers.
- No note covers how server creatures are drawn on the client, staff report screens, or a real phone load test.
