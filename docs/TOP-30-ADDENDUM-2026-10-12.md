## Combat axes

1. Two separate mitigation axes: armour against blades, resist against spells. AzerothCore: physical through armour/(armour+K) capped 75%; spell through school resist R/(R+C) capped 75% then a partial draw in 10% bins; a multi-school spell takes the target's lowest resist. ACE: every armour piece carries a per-damage-type multiplier -2..2, so a piece can be weak to a type; pieces sum per body part. EQEmu: a target 20+ levels above resists nearly all. | Plate is a wall against swords and a liability against fire; chosen per piece. Gear must enter stepDuel first. | AzerothCore, ACE, EQEmu, B | hard | MG012/out/magic-spells-powers/azerothcore-azerothcore-wotlk.deep.md; SK003/out/character-stats-skills/ACEmulator-ACE.deep.md; MG002/out/magic-spells-powers/EQEmu.deep.md
2. Damage kinds with different side effects. Veloren: slashing drains stamina = 0.5 x damage with shortfall as health; crushing turns mitigated damage into stagger; piercing ignores armour by a penetration 0..1; block soak before percent reduction. | Axe tires you, hammer staggers you, spear goes through plate. | Veloren, B | hard | deep/combat-sim/veloren-veloren.deep.md
3. Armour taxes casting. Ryzom: spell success minus armour malus x skill; Crawl: armour and shield penalty added to spell failure through a cubic. | The knight in plate casts badly. | Ryzom, Crawl, B | easy | MG010/out/magic-spells-powers/ryzom-ryzomcore.deep.md; MG005/.../crawl-crawl.deep.md
4. Build-your-own spells from bricks. Ryzom: ordered bricks with signed costs; relative level (skill + credit - 2 x cost)/10 clipped +-50 into a success table (crit 1.1x, success, partial band, failure, critical failure). Daggerfall Unity (MIT): cost per effect = offset + A x start + B x per-level, skill discount (110-skill)/400, floor 5; pool cost and shop price from one list. Maps onto specials-as-rows: template + parameters = a brick list. | Players make their own spells; a greedy build is cheaper but fails harder. | Ryzom B, Daggerfall A | hard | MG010/out/magic-spells-powers/ryzom-ryzomcore.deep.md; MG010/.../daggerfall-unity.deep.md
5. One roll decides the hit; the defender's state gates the ladder. AzerothCore order: evade, miss, dodge, parry, block, glancing, crushing, crit, normal; a casting or stunned defender has zero dodge/parry/block width; glancing only for a player hitting a higher-level creature. AAEmu and OpenDAoC: only front-arc attacks can be parried or dodged; strafing +30% forced miss. | Stun a caster and your hits land; flank a brute and its parry is gone. One roll keeps replay deterministic. | B | medium | Q073/out/combat-sim/azerothcore-azerothcore-wotlk.deep.md; V006/out/combat-sim/AAEmu-AAEmu.deep.md; deep/combat-sim/OpenDAoC-OpenDAoC-Core.deep.md
6. Luck is a defence; a crowd makes you easier to hit. rAthena: lucky dodge first, crit always hits, crit chance cut by 2 x target LUK, evasion reduced by number of attackers. | Pairs with the attacker cap: being swarmed is dangerous. | rAthena, B | easy | deep/combat-sim/rathena-rathena.deep.md
7. Immunity by tier, resist by curve. ModernUO: poison tier vs creature immuneTier, boss variant = +1. EQEmu partial: a failed roll lands 150 x (chance - roll)/chance, zero at a third of chance. | Bosses shrug low-grade effects by data; resist is a slope. | B | easy | SE007/ModernUO.status-effects.md; MG002/.../EQEmu.deep.md
8. CC diminishing returns and a post-CC safe window. AzerothCore: 100/50/25/immune, reset 15 s after last aura, shorter new CC refused, world bosses never advance a ladder. OpenDAoC: immunity starts when the CC ends, 60 s, separate per kind; a refused CC still interrupts. | Chain stuns peter out. | B | easy | MG012/...azerothcore...; MG003/out/magic-spells-powers/OpenDAoC-OpenDAoC-Core.deep.md
9. Threat as data, weighted by distance. DOLSharp: target = max threat x min(500/distance, 1); heals add threat only if already on the table; miss adds 1; dead pet's threat to owner. 10. Hit stop and a stagger ladder with numbers. Ikemen-GO (MIT): attacker/victim pause frames, victim freeze only if not already in hit-stun, one shake object, 3-frame white flash, guard break 20 frames red.
