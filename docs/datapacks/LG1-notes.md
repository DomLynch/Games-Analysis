# LG1 notes: legal look sources for legendary games (research only, nothing downloaded)

## Diablo 1
- Box: diasurgical-DevilutionX README line 27 says the game needs DIABDAT.MPQ from a CD, a GOG copy or Battle.net; the same line names the shareware spawn.mpq as an alternative. The box has UI .clx art, text data and palettes under assets/ and mods/hf, but no monster or tile sprite sheets.
- Legal route: README line 27 cites the Blizzard shareware installer at http://ftp.blizzard.com/pub/demos/diablosw.exe. This is the rights holder's own free demo (category 1). I did not open or download it.
- Not used: the spawn.mpq on the devilutionx-assets GitHub release is a fan-hosted copy, so it is out. A DiabloBytes page (fetched) calls itself an unofficial fan site and says the shareware "was freely distributed by Blizzard in 1996-1997".
- Owner route: GOG Diablo (paid) gives the full DIABDAT.MPQ. Owner-copy-only.
- Free substitute: Kenney Isometric Miniature Dungeon (CC0, 72 tiles at 256x512, 14.2 MB zip). Source: https://opengameart.org/content/isometric-miniature-dungeon (fetched). It is environment art, not monsters, and it is listed under "Art for a Diablo-like RPG".

## Diablo 2
- Box: OpenDiablo2-OpenDiablo2 README lines 30 and 49-51 require a legally purchased Diablo 2 and Lord of Destruction install. Depth-3 search found no MPQ in the box.
- Legal route: none free. Searches show only time-limited trials (Xbox March 2022; PC Oct 2022 to Jan 2023) and no free game data. Unofficial private servers are excluded.
- Result: owner-copy-only. Free substitute: none verified with a Diablo II style. Kenney's isometric dungeon (CC0) is the nearest isometric look, but it does not match Diablo II art.

## Ultima Online
- Box: ClassicUO, crossuo-crossuo and ZaneDubya-UltimaXNA are clients without UO data. UltimaXNA README line 14 says it "does not include the Ultima Online resource files". xrip-uo-client carries a RESTRICTED note (decompilation of Origin's client), so it is not used. ModernUO is a server. Their CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md files were not followed.
- Legal route: uo.com (fetched via its redirect to http://uo.com/) shows a "Play For Free Now" link and a "Client Download" link. The page gives no trial length or terms and no date, so the offer is unverified. The 14-day trial in 2007 to 2009 press (EA press release, uo.com trial links) is too old to rely on.
- Not used: Shattered Moon's "Classic Ultima Online" is a fan project based on Ultima 4, not a rights-holder release. Fan client packs and shards are excluded.
- Free substitute: none verified for UO. Kenney isometric packs (CC0) are the nearest open-licence look, but they do not match UO art.

## Ultima VII / Ultima series
- Box: exult-exult data/ holds Exult's own UI shapes, fonts, exult0.pal and midisfx.flx. Its README names the GOG installer for Ultima VII (not fetched). The world art is missing.
- Free route: Wikipedia (fetched) says EA released Worlds of Ultima: The Savage Empire as freeware through GOG on 18 June 2012. Martian Dreams was offered the same way (search result only). GOG store listing: https://www.gog.com/en/game/worlds_of_ultima_the_savage_empire. Current price and status are not verified.
- Paid route: GOG "Ultima 7 The Complete Edition" (DRM-free, EA copyright). Owner-copy-only for Ultima VII.
- Excluded: Ultima IV copies bundled with magazines, because EA sent takedowns from 2011 and the copy is not open-licence. Origin's "On the House" promos (Ultima VIII, Underworld 1 and 2) are time-limited and expired.
- Free substitute: none verified for Ultima VII art.
