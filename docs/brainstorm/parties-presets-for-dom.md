# Parties, guilds, chat and presence: presets for Dom

NOTHING IS BUILT. This is a design proposal only. No code, data, server or test exists for any of it. All numbers are our own starting guesses, to be tuned on a phone. Full detail: parties-brainstorm-v2.md.

Whatever preset you pick, three things never change: the server checks everything, there is one ledger, and at most 3 attackers hit one target. A party can be bigger than 3 (extras wait on a reserve bench) or exactly 3. The Pit is not touched.

## The 5 presets

1. Solo friendly. Nobody is forced to group. Parties are small (up to 3), each attacker is credited alone, loot is free for all, no guilds. Chat is zone, party and private. Feels like playing alone with the option of company.

2. Duo and trio. Party size equals fight size, so the 3-on-1 fight is the main loop. Shared XP is weighted by level with a small group bonus (about +10% for two, +20% for three). Loot goes to a random eligible member. If a player drops, their seat is held for about 25 seconds. Feels like a tight crew.

3. Guild heavy. Guilds with five ranks (leader, admin, officer, member, recruit), rank permissions, guild chat, a global lobby, friends lists. Parties have 3 attackers plus 1 reserve who can swap in. Loot rotates. Feels like a persistent community.

4. Casual queue. A matchmaking queue for quick pickup fights: it matches by rank, widens the gap the longer you wait, and always gives you a match or a clear reason. Small chat, tight spam limits. Feels like tap and fight.

5. Locked down. For younger players or noisy periods. No global lobby, short messages (80 characters), strict spam limits, friends-only visibility by default, shadow mute (a muted player sees their own messages, nobody else does). Feels safe and quiet.

## The 6 questions

1. Can a party be bigger than 3 (reserve bench behind the 3 attacker seats), or is party size capped at 3?
2. Should shared XP be worth more than solo (a visible group bonus), or an equal total split?
3. Do guilds exist at launch, or only party, chat and presence first?
4. Should a global lobby chat exist, or only zone, party, guild and private?
5. When someone disconnects mid-fight, hold their seat for a grace window (we suggest 25 seconds) or drop them at once?
6. Do you want a matchmaking queue at launch, or invite-based play only?
