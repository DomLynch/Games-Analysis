# GL focus (gods-legends-favour), from Strategy 2026-10-12. Read after TASK.md for every GL group.
Frankendom mixes pantheons and 700 legends (Norse, Greek, Indian, more): Thor, Poseidon, Loki, Hades... Content rule (legends-rule): myth, NOT scripture; folk heroes only as honourable figures. Code only from MIT/Apache/BSD donors; GPL/AGPL/restricted = shape only. Acceptance idea: a new god or legend = data ROWS only, no code.
Give TABLE rows (name | effect | formula | number ranges | gain/loss events | decay | file:line) for:
1. Favour/piety per god as a resource: gain/loss events (kills, offerings, oaths kept/broken, desecration), decay.
2. Blessings and curses as timed status rows on the one CC/effect ladder.
3. Patron choice and switching: conversion cost, apostasy penalty, god anger (Crawl-style wrath).
4. Divine domains/aspects as tags on legends and items (storm, sea, trickster, underworld, war, fate) driving damage/resist tables.
5. Boons at favour thresholds (ranks, not random).
6. Legend signature powers as special rows per named opponent per rank (power id, cooldown, rank scaling).
7. Shapeshifting/transformation as a temporary catalogue swap (body family, moves, stats), duration, break rules.
8. Avatars/summoned aspects under summon limits.
9. Fate, luck, oaths: luck stat feed, oath contracts with a server-checked condition and a forfeit.
10. Underworld/death rules (corpse run, resurrection cost, death-penalty tiers, what a necromancer can do with a corpse).
11. Divine/legendary mounts as rewards.
12. Relics/artifacts as unique items (one in the world, ownership transfer on kill, decay or curse on holder).
13. Temples/shrines as zone rows (place, service, offering).
14. Pantheon factions and realm pride for PvP sides (DAoC realms, Shadowbane nations); reputation/faction standing; titles/epithets as career rows.
Feeds: catalogue rows, tuning rows for specials, Core items/career (favour, titles, oaths as ledger rows), zone rows, CC ladder.
