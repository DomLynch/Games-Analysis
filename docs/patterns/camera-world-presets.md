# Camera presets to try on your phone

We built five ways the world camera could feel. You pick on your phone by adding a flag to the preview link: `?cam=A`, `?cam=B`, `?cam=C`, `?cam=D` or `?cam=E`. Switching blends over under half a second, so you can compare in place. Only the world camera changes. The Pit camera stays exactly as it is, no matter which preset you pick. All numbers are our own starting guesses for a 375x812 portrait screen, and you can tune them.

## The five presets

- **A, Shoulder Classic (default).** The camera sits just behind and to one side of your hero, like a third-person action game. It flips to the other shoulder when a wall is near, pulls in smoothly when something blocks the view, and follows calmly. This is the baseline.
- **B, Duel Frame.** Built for fights. As enemies join (up to 3), the camera backs off a little and drifts toward the middle so everyone stays in view. Hits give a short, small shake.
- **C, Runner.** For exploring. The camera pulls back and widens when you sprint, and looks slightly ahead of where you are going. Feels fast.
- **D, Cinematic Lag.** Heavier and filmic. The camera trails behind with a visible spring, fades walls that get in the way, and settles slowly after you flick the view. Pretty, but might feel floaty.
- **E, Tight Pocket.** For corridors and small rooms. Close and a bit high, hugs the hero, and avoids swinging around. Meant to be applied per zone rather than everywhere.

## How to try it

1. Open the preview link on your iPhone with `?cam=A`.
2. Play about ten minutes, then repeat with B to E.
3. A debug overlay shows which camera parts are active and their numbers, so you can tell us what to change.

## Six questions for you

1. Which shoulder should be the default, or should it follow whichever thumb is dragging the view? Do you want a left-handed mode?
2. In a fight, should your hero stay slightly off to one side, or move toward the centre as enemies arrive?
3. How much camera lag do you like: crisp (about a tenth of a second) or filmic (about a quarter second)?
4. Should dragging always look around freely, or only while you hold a button?
5. When a wall is behind you, should the camera pull in, fade the wall, or turn to the open side?
6. Is a small shake and zoom pulse on heavy hits welcome, or should the default be calm with shake as an option?
