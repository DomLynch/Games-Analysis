## 1 Design pillars
- Iterate on a proven formula: shipped just over a year after Heroes I as refinement; the studio method was iteration. [https://www.filfre.net/2023/02/17/]
- Lineage from King's Bounty: Van Caneghem wanted to revisit it with better technology; Heroes I added a true strategic wargame, up to eight heroes, castles producing troops. [https://www.filfre.net/2021/12/24/]
- Intended as a pure, chess-like strategy game; hero names, portraits and levelling crept in and players loved them, so Heroes II doubled down. [https://www.filfre.net/2023/02/17/]
- Tuned by playtest: Van Caneghem adjusted movement distance and turn length until testers argued about strategy, a sign the game was ready (Heroes I). [https://www.filfre.net/2021/12/24/]
- Short sessions and accessible tone: maps seldom exceed a few hours, bright non-grim art. [https://www.filfre.net/2021/12/24/]
- Add variety more than complexity: two new factions, 14 secondary skills, a campaign, a scenario editor. [https://www.filfre.net/2023/02/17/]

## 2 Why players loved it
- "One more week": a seven-day cycle with castles replenished weekly; players lost whole nights. [http://www.gamebanshee.com/kd4k]
- Develop the castle quickly to field higher-tier creatures that dominate encounters. [http://www.gamebanshee.com/kd4k]
- Stack-based combat: simple but clever; mass cheap units can beat stronger ones (halflings example). [http://www.gamebanshee.com/kd4k]
- Heroes as characters: levelling and secondary skills create bonding. [https://www.filfre.net/2023/02/17/]
- Necromancer snowballing: raise skeletons from defeated enemies so winning grows the army. [https://gameinformer.com/b/news/archive/2010/12/29/a-look-back-at-heroes-of-might-amp-magic-ii]
- Eight-hero cap limits micromanagement; polished UI and light art that has aged well. [https://www.filfre.net/2023/02/17/]
- Scenario editor used for official maps spawned a long-lived fan community. [https://www.filfre.net/2023/02/17/]
- Critical reception: CGW 5/5, called "nearly perfect"; PC Gamer US 1996 best turn-based strategy. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes_of_Might_and_Magic_II]

## 3 Pacing and progression feel
- Early: pick a faction, build and recruit; weekly growth means early build order matters. [http://www.gamebanshee.com/kd4k]
- Mid: heroes gain primary and secondary skills (Basic/Advanced/Expert), spell points replace memorisation. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes_of_Might_and_Magic_II]
- Late: endgame can be anticlimactic once victory is certain. [https://www.filfre.net/2023/02/17/]
- Campaign: choose good or evil path; victories give lasting bonuses carried into later scenarios; pre-scenario starting bonus choice. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes_of_Might_and_Magic_II]
- Many scenarios finish in a few hours, some with alternative victory conditions. [https://www.filfre.net/2023/02/17/]

## 4 Signature features and systems
- Six factions (Knight, Barbarian, Sorceress, Warlock, Necromancer, Wizard). [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes_of_Might_and_Magic_II]
- Creature upgrades (first appearing in the series here per a retrospective). [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes_of_Might_and_Magic_II]
- Smaller combat hexes, about twice as many units on a battlefield. [https://www.filfre.net/2023/02/17/]
- Larger map range, scripted triggers, scenario editor. [https://www.filfre.net/2023/02/17/]
- Expansion The Price of Loyalty (1997) with four campaigns; Gold edition with 30 community maps. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes_of_Might_and_Magic_II]

## 5 Known flaws and regrets
- Weak computer AI; hard scenarios rely on outnumbering the player. [https://www.filfre.net/2023/02/17/]
- Puzzle-like scenarios requiring trial and error; anticlimactic endgame; necromancy possibly unbalanced; forgettable campaign story. [https://www.filfre.net/2023/02/17/]
- Contemporary critic complaints: no wait function; cannot control a hero whose units are all disabled. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes_of_Might_and_Magic_II]
- Heroes I had overpowered Warlock dragons and anticlimactic endgame. [https://www.filfre.net/2021/12/24/]
- Designer regrets in the first person: UNSOURCED (none found).

## 6 What Frankendom should take
1. A fixed weekly/daily rhythm of replenishment gives players a reason to return on schedule (zone spawns or vendor stock refreshing on a cycle). INFERRED from the weekly-growth loop [http://www.gamebanshee.com/kd4k].
2. Stacks as the unit of power: let cheap units in numbers threaten a strong one, which suits many-vs-one fights. SOURCED mechanic (halflings) [http://www.gamebanshee.com/kd4k]; transfer INFERRED.
3. Tiered skill upgrades (Basic to Expert) as a readable progression ladder per character. SOURCED mechanic [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes_of_Might_and_Magic_II]; transfer INFERRED.
4. Progression that snowballs from winning (necromancy-style) but needs a catch-up or cap to avoid the anticlimactic endgame flagged by critics. Snowball SOURCED; fix INFERRED [https://www.filfre.net/2023/02/17/].
5. Ship the content editor and keep session length short; carry bonuses between sessions. SOURCED (editor, short maps, campaign carry-over) [https://www.filfre.net/2023/02/17/].
