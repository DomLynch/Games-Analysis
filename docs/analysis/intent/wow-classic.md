# World of Warcraft (2004, classic era): design-intent brief

Method note: fetched pages were read in full or in part; items marked (search snippet) came from search-result summaries, not a full page read. Wording is paraphrased.

## 1 Design pillars
- Beat EverQuest by being more accessible and newcomer-friendly; many Blizzard developers, Rob Pardo included, were EverQuest players, and management voted by near-unanimity to pivot from the cancelled Nomad project to a Warcraft MMO [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_of_Warcraft] [https://mein-mmo.de/wow-entwicklung-anzahl-quests-rob-pardo/]
- Gameplay first: design around the fun core rather than tech limits, even changing Warcraft III lore for balance and fun [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/gdc-blizzard-s-core-game-design-concepts]
- Easy to learn, hard to master; build the in-depth experience first, then make it approachable. Pardo called WoW a hardcore game that is simply more accessible [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/gdc-blizzard-s-core-game-design-concepts] [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/agc-blizzard-s-pardo-on-i-wow-i-s-success]
- Open world with optional quests that guide progression and spread players across zones to avoid "player collision" [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_of_Warcraft]
- Make everything feel overpowered and turn punishments into bonuses (rest system reframed from an XP cut to a bonus) [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/gdc-blizzard-s-core-game-design-concepts]
- Core plus casual "donut": serve both hardcore centre and casual ring (Pardo, 2006) [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/agc-blizzard-s-pardo-on-i-wow-i-s-success]

## 2 Why players loved it
- Quest-driven levelling with a seamless open world (no loading screens), reduced downtime, lighter death penalty and the rested bonus were singled out by critics; Metacritic 93 [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_of_Warcraft]
- Beta testers ran out of quests and the game felt broken, so the team added roughly ten times the planned quests; that depth of content became a launch strength [https://mein-mmo.de/wow-entwicklung-anzahl-quests-rob-pardo/]
- Social friction: hard content and a hostile world pushed players to find groups by chat, and that friction welded groups and guilds together (Classic-team developers, 2019 retrospective) [https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/news/wow-classic-interview-for-computer-bild-with-brian-birmingham-and-omar-gonzalez/]
- Levelling was treated as the core of the game, and free-form quests let players find their own route and story [https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/news/wow-classic-interview-for-computer-bild-with-brian-birmingham-and-omar-gonzalez/]
- Even weak-looking creatures were threats to a novice, so leaving the starting city felt scary [https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/news/wow-classic-interview-for-computer-bild-with-brian-birmingham-and-omar-gonzalez/]
- Stylised art and low system requirements reached a wider audience [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/agc-blizzard-s-pardo-on-i-wow-i-s-success]
- Pardo's rule of thumb: the game should be fun within the first five minutes (Joi Ito's account of a 2006 dinner) [https://joi.ito.com/weblog/_markdown/2006/05/23/the_wisdom_of_r.md] (search snippet)

## 3 Pacing and progression feel
- First hour: free trial accounts were capped at level 20, so the early game was built to hook before payment [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_of_Warcraft]. Quests guide a newcomer from zone to zone [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_of_Warcraft]
- Mid game: dungeons for up to five players; group-finding was done by chat with minimal tools, which made organising tedious [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_of_Warcraft] [https://massivelyop.com/2026/06/25/casually-classic-what-were-vanilla-world-of-warcrafts-weaknesses/] (search snippet). Level-40 mount cost roughly 100 gold in classic, a visible money goal [https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/mounts/] (search snippet; figures vary by patch)
- Late game: raids up to 40 players and PvP battlegrounds with honor and token rewards for gear [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_of_Warcraft]. A retrospective says endgame was mostly raiding and leaned on numbers and buffs more than skill [https://massivelyop.com/2026/06/25/casually-classic-what-were-vanilla-world-of-warcrafts-weaknesses/] (search snippet)
- Flight taxis limit instant travel on purpose so the world feels bigger and encourages social contact [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/agc-blizzard-s-pardo-on-i-wow-i-s-success]

## 4 Signature features and systems
- Quest hubs with optional guidance, rested XP bonus, class identity ("concentrated coolness": fewer, deeper classes) [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/agc-blizzard-s-pardo-on-i-wow-i-s-success]
- Dungeons deliberately not soloable; no solo instances [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/agc-blizzard-s-pardo-on-i-wow-i-s-success]
- Prestige armour sets rather than customisable gear, because art time is limited; no mounted combat because value did not justify cost [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/agc-blizzard-s-pardo-on-i-wow-i-s-success]
- Simple HUD; auction house reached via an NPC rather than a button [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/agc-blizzard-s-pardo-on-i-wow-i-s-success]
- Battlegrounds as set-objective PvP; winners earn more honor than losers [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_of_Warcraft]
- Later Classic team pillars (not 2004 intent, retro-fitted): protect social experiences, approachable and familiar, world as main character [https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wotlk/news/wrath-of-the-lich-king-classic-dev-updates-naxx-buffs-itemization-social-play/] (search snippet, secondary copy of Blizzard blog)

## 5 Known flaws and regrets
- Launch: some quests were bugged and impossible; crowded zones had no mobs left, forcing players to take turns [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_of_Warcraft]
- Critics: few group quests (felt solo-oriented), repetitive drop quests, dungeon quests unfriendly to newcomers, limited customisation [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_of_Warcraft]
- PvP honor ladder was exploitable: account sharing let rotating players keep one character queued nonstop for rank gear; removed before vanilla ended [https://www.engadget.com/2014-04-22-the-mistakes-of-the-world-of-warcraft.html]. Later Classic removed honor decay as a grind [https://massivelyop.com/2023/11/29/world-of-warcrafts-classic-servers-are-removing-honor-decay-altogether-with-the-next-weekly-maintenance/] (search snippet)
- Raid lockouts: whoever entered first could lock out others, causing friction [https://mein-mmo.de/en/wow-5-schreckliche-dinge-classic,189414/] (search snippet, machine-translated)
- Original rest system cut XP, which beta testers disliked, hence the bonus reframe [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/gdc-blizzard-s-core-game-design-concepts]

## 6 What Frankendom should take
1. Reframe penalties as bonuses (e.g. fresh-fighter or rested boost that decays to normal, never a malus). SOURCED [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/gdc-blizzard-s-core-game-design-concepts]
2. Make the player feel strong in the first five minutes; first duel should be winnable and flashy, then raise threat by zone. SOURCED in principle (fun in five minutes, overpowered feel) [https://joi.ito.com/weblog/_markdown/2006/05/23/the_wisdom_of_r.md]; the duel application is INFERRED.
3. Plan 10x the content you think the zone loop needs; players exhaust quests/loot targets faster than designers expect. SOURCED [https://mein-mmo.de/wow-entwicklung-anzahl-quests-rob-pardo/]
4. Spread players across zones (many-vs-one fights need room; avoid mob-camping where targets run out). SOURCED on "player collision"; many-vs-one mapping INFERRED [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_of_Warcraft]
5. If you add rank-based PvP rewards, make rank non-shareable and non-grindable (anti-account-rotation, no punishing decay). INFERRED from the honor-ladder failure [https://www.engadget.com/2014-04-22-the-mistakes-of-the-world-of-warcraft.html]
