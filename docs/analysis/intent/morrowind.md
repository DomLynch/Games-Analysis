## 1 Design pillars
- Free-form play: Howard named the "free-form experience" a core element of the series; the main plot was a way to introduce a "distracting crosscurrent" of factions and characters, per Rolston. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_III:_Morrowind]
- A huge, hand-built, explorable world; objects were hand-crafted, not randomly generated as in Arena and Daggerfall. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_III:_Morrowind]
- Single-player only, no multiplayer. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_III:_Morrowind]
- Multiple valid character paths via many faction and side quests; the seven main-plot tasks could be done in many orders. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_III:_Morrowind]
- Give players tools they can break (spellcrafting) and trust them with the result. [https://www.gamebanshee.com/xqfx3]
- Tabletop-style, elaborate worldbuilding by Rolston and an alien setting by Kirkbride. [https://avclub.com/how-bethesda-made-its-brilliant-broken-morrowind-secre-1833655640]

## 2 Why players loved it
- Rolston's explanation: the appeal is that there is "too much there" (his words, per the interview write-up), like jazz where not every note suits every listener. [https://gamersnexus.net/gg/1418-ken-rolston-morrowind-interview-ecgc]
- Scale, ambition and strangeness; rough edges are part of the charm. [https://avclub.com/how-bethesda-made-its-brilliant-broken-morrowind-secre-1833655640]
- Reviews praised scope, visuals, weather and day/night, and the skill system; GameSpot called plain exploration possibly the best thing about it. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_III:_Morrowind]
- Spellcrafting gave surprising, personal discoveries (e.g. a "flight" spell that is really a big jump buff). [https://www.gamebanshee.com/xqfx3]
- Quirky quest design, e.g. "Hentus Needs Pants". [https://gamersnexus.net/gg/1418-ken-rolston-morrowind-interview-ecgc]
- 4M+ sold by Aug 2005; best-selling Xbox RPG of 2002. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_III:_Morrowind]

## 3 Pacing and progression feel
- First hour: begins with release from prison, then character creation (race, class, birthsign). [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_III:_Morrowind] An early dead-elf journal near Seyda Neen teaches spell experimentation. [https://www.gamebanshee.com/xqfx3]
- Early difficulty is harsh: new characters often cannot beat weak creatures, and miss-rolls feel unfair (critic opinion); workarounds are poorly signposted. [https://www.shamusyoung.com/twentysidedtale/?p=28747]
- Skills improve by use, paid training or books; attributes only on level-up (pick three). [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_III:_Morrowind]
- World does not level-scale: enemies and treasure mostly ignore player level, and strong gear can be found early; the player ends up trivialising nearly any threat, including the final boss (critic opinion). [https://www.shamusyoung.com/twentysidedtale/?p=28747]
- Fast travel is available for a fee by teleport, boat or silt strider. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_III:_Morrowind]

## 4 Signature features and systems
- Construction Set (the team spent the first year building it), enabling mods; about 100 man-years of work. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_III:_Morrowind]
- Use-based skills, birthsigns, chop/slash/thrust combat with hidden roll modifiers. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_III:_Morrowind]
- Custom spell creation. [https://www.gamebanshee.com/xqfx3]
- Hand-placed content and fixed-level regions. [https://www.shamusyoung.com/twentysidedtale/?p=28747]

## 5 Known flaws and regrets
- Combat called simplistic and tedious; no enemy health bars at launch (patched about a month later). [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_III:_Morrowind]
- Journal widely disliked; main plot criticised for lacking urgency; glitches and heavy system requirements. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_III:_Morrowind]
- Messy, buggy, obtuse and confusing to newcomers. [https://avclub.com/how-bethesda-made-its-brilliant-broken-morrowind-secre-1833655640]
- Rolston: the team did more than it could polish; it was close to a miracle that it works. He frames the lack of polish as part of the appeal rather than a regret. [https://gamersnexus.net/gg/1418-ken-rolston-morrowind-interview-ecgc]
- Original plan (dynamic blight destroying cities, whole province) was scaled back because tech could not support it. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_III:_Morrowind]
- Open systems had absurd side effects (levitation, sprint-loading); later games simplified or cut spellcrafting. [https://www.gamebanshee.com/xqfx3]
- Crunch and studio uncertainty during development. [https://avclub.com/how-bethesda-made-its-brilliant-broken-morrowind-secre-1833655640]

## 6 What Frankendom should take
1. Fixed-level zones with a visible danger gradient (not scaling to the player) make the world feel objective and reward knowledge. SOURCED as the design trade [https://www.shamusyoung.com/twentysidedtale/?p=28747]; but the same source says early-game punishment is a flaw, so signpost danger clearly in Frankendom. Signposting advice is INFERRED.
2. Give the player a tool that can be abused (a build/combo system) rather than only fixed abilities; discovery drives retention. SOURCED [https://www.gamebanshee.com/xqfx3]; mapping to fight moves is INFERRED.
3. Do not let hidden dice decide hits in a duel; the critique of roll-based misses fits a fast action fighter badly. Critique SOURCED [https://www.shamusyoung.com/twentysidedtale/?p=28747] (and Wikipedia: combat criticised [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_III:_Morrowind]); the rule for Frankendom is INFERRED.
4. Use-based skill growth (improve what you do) gives readable progression without a level treadmill. SOURCED mechanic [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_III:_Morrowind]; fit to a duel game is INFERRED.
5. Cheap fast-travel nodes between zones keep a large map from becoming a chore. SOURCED mechanic [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_III:_Morrowind]; INFERRED value.
6. Ship health bars and basic combat feedback at launch (the game added them a month later). SOURCED fact [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_III:_Morrowind]; lesson INFERRED.
