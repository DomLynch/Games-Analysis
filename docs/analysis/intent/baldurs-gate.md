## 1 Design pillars
- Bring the depth and rules fidelity of the Gold Box D&D games forward with an RTS-style interface. [https://www.theringer.com/2018/12/21/18150363/baldurs-gate-bioware-1998-video-games]
- Real-time with pause so full AD&D 2nd edition rules work without click wars; it reconciled Ray Muzyka's turn-based taste with Ohlen's RTS taste. [https://www.theringer.com/2018/12/21/18150363/baldurs-gate-bioware-1998-video-games] [https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/the-history-of-baldurs-gate]
- A tabletop-feeling party: distinct companion personalities and controlled chaos, so the game feels different by party make-up. [https://www.theringer.com/2018/12/21/18150363/baldurs-gate-bioware-1998-video-games] [https://www.pcgamesn.com/article/baldur-s-gate-enhanced-edition-interview-trent-oster-building-bioware-06-mark-2]
- More than one way to reach each goal (Wasteland influence). [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldur%27s_Gate_(video_game)]
- Story rooted in a real pen-and-paper campaign run by Ohlen. [https://www.pcgamesn.com/article/baldur-s-gate-enhanced-edition-interview-trent-oster-building-bioware-06-mark-2]
- Engine origin: Infinity Engine began as a prototype for an RTS/multiplayer project (Battleground: Infinity), repurposed after Interplay suggested a D&D license. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinity_Engine] [https://gamesradar.com/making-of-baldurs-gate]

## 2 Why players loved it
- Combat that was fast and often deadly at low level, a thrill even at some cost of precision (retrospective podcast description). [https://hu.player.fm/series/retro-spectives/e128-baldurs-gate]
- Pause feature made battles a joy and avoided clicking wars (fan review). [https://archive.rpgamer.com/games/ad_d/bg/reviews/bgstrev1.html]
- Companions and reputation: evil/good companions leave or turn on you depending on your reputation; reputation also changes prices and quest availability. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldur%27s_Gate_(video_game)]
- Lavish hand-rendered backgrounds; the city felt vast and dangerous per PC Gamer. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldur%27s_Gate_(video_game)]
- Word of mouth, helped by BioWare's open community communication. [https://www.theringer.com/2018/12/21/18150363/baldurs-gate-bioware-1998-video-games]
- Writing volume: roughly 800,000 words, about 70 percent by Kristjanson. [https://www.theringer.com/2018/12/21/18150363/baldurs-gate-bioware-1998-video-games]

## 3 Pacing and progression feel
- Structure: prologue plus seven chapters; some areas locked until a chapter; fog of war re-hides areas. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldur%27s_Gate_(video_game)]
- Travel: characters tire after a full in-game day; long trips and camping can trigger ambushes. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldur%27s_Gate_(video_game)]
- Early hours: low-level, fast, deadly fights. [https://hu.player.fm/series/retro-spectives/e128-baldurs-gate] The Enhanced Edition team kept original difficulty, urging players to save often and level early. [https://www.pcgamesn.com/article/baldur-s-gate-enhanced-edition-interview-trent-oster-building-bioware-06-mark-2]
- Mid and late game feel, XP curve and level cap: UNSOURCED (only fan forum claims found; omitted).
- Time pressure led to simpler areas and design than planned. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldur%27s_Gate_(video_game)]

## 4 Signature features and systems
- Real-time with pause plus auto-pause triggers and queued orders. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldur%27s_Gate_(video_game)]
- Up to five companions plus protagonist; multiplayer up to six. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldur%27s_Gate_(video_game)]
- AD&D 2nd edition rules automated (dice, stats, multiclass restrictions). [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldur%27s_Gate_(video_game)]
- Reputation system. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldur%27s_Gate_(video_game)]
- Pre-rendered 2D scrolling backgrounds with sprite characters, 800x600 original limit. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinity_Engine]
- Reused engine for Icewind Dale, Planescape: Torment. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinity_Engine]

## 5 Known flaws and regrets
- Pathfinding was the main criticism (Maximum PC); pixel-sprite characters panned. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldur%27s_Gate_(video_game)]
- A novice team underestimated scope, with heavy crunch; Oster said BG2 was when they finally knew how to make a game. [https://www.theringer.com/2018/12/21/18150363/baldurs-gate-bioware-1998-video-games]
- Ohlen called BG1 a learning experience: small team meant he did hands-on scripting and placement, leaving less time for story, puzzles and balance. [https://pbg.bgforge.net/features/wonder/wonder030200/index.html]
- Per-area individual rendering greatly lengthened production. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldur%27s_Gate_(video_game)]
- Empty wilderness maps: only forum complaints found; UNSOURCED, omitted from advice.
- Shipping: late, supply shortages into Jan 1999; Interplay forecasted very low sales (UK predicted zero), actual first run of 50k sold out. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldur%27s_Gate_(video_game)]

2. Many-vs-one fights benefit from formation and positioning mattering, as in BG. SOURCED for BG positioning (dosdays description via search) [https://dosdays.co.uk/topics/Games/games_baldur.php]; Frankendom use INFERRED.
3. Reputation as a persistent system changing prices, who helps you and who attacks. SOURCED mechanics [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldur%27s_Gate_(video_game)]; mapping onto zones/factions INFERRED.
4. Zones gated by chapter with fog-of-war reveal to meter progression. SOURCED mechanic; application INFERRED.
