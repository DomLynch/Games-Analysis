# RuneScape (2001-2004 era): design-intent brief

Method note: primary developer material for 2001-2004 is thin online. Most gameplay detail for that era is UNSOURCED beyond what is cited below.

## 1 Design pillars
- Accessible, affordable, enjoyable online RPG that works in a browser, filling a gap between weak free games and expensive subscription RPGs (Paul Gower, 2010) [https://www.engadget.com/2010-12-07-the-game-archaeologist-and-the-forbidden-runescape-words-with-p.html]
- Low barrier: a small plug-in only, playable in short sessions from any machine such as a library PC [https://www.pcgamesn.com/longest-game-making-runescape]
- A graphical MUD that stands apart; origin was DeviousMud (1998), released as RuneScape beta 4 January 2001 [https://www.engadget.com/2010-12-07-the-game-archaeologist-and-the-forbidden-runescape-words-with-p.html] [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuneScape]
- Free tier plus paid membership: ads failed after the dotcom bust, membership launched February 2002 [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuneScape] [https://www.engadget.com/2010-12-07-the-game-archaeologist-and-the-forbidden-runescape-words-with-p.html]
- Layer content continuously (weekly updates) rather than rework foundations [https://www.pcgamesn.com/longest-game-making-runescape]
- Medieval fantasy tone drawn from tabletop and board games, with quests valuing entertainment over strict canon [https://www.pcgamesn.com/longest-game-making-runescape]

## 2 Why players loved it
- Free to try, instantly playable in a browser; Gower credits the scale and variety of content for wide appeal [https://www.engadget.com/2010-12-07-the-game-archaeologist-and-the-forbidden-runescape-words-with-p.html]
- Over 5,000 members signed up in the first week of membership and 1 million accounts followed within a year [https://www.pcgamesn.com/longest-game-making-runescape]
- Skill-based progression: XP from doing, with higher levels unlocking resources, gear and areas [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuneScape]
- Sandbox feel where players set their own goals (PC Gamer description, as cited) [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_School_RuneScape]
- The Wilderness gave a high-stakes PvP place where the winner takes unprotected items (introduced 13 August 2001) [https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Wilderness]
- Classic had a class system briefly; it was removed because class differences were too minor [https://classic.runescape.wiki/w/RuneScape_Classic]

## 3 Pacing and progression feel
- First hour: low commitment, point-and-click controls, quests that award XP, items and new areas [https://classic.runescape.wiki/w/RuneScape_Classic]
- Mid game: levelling skills (gathering and combat) opens new resources, equipment and areas; members' content such as herblore adds breadth [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuneScape] [https://www.pcgamesn.com/longest-game-making-runescape]
- Late game: critics later praised progression but criticised combat and grinding (2011 MMORPG.com, as cited) [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuneScape]. Specific 2001-2004 pacing numbers: UNSOURCED.
- Rewards arrived through steady weekly additions [https://www.pcgamesn.com/longest-game-making-runescape]

## 4 Signature features and systems
- Skills-based advancement, quest points, player trading and shops, dueling [https://classic.runescape.wiki/w/RuneScape_Classic]
- Wilderness PvP: skulls forfeit all items on death; the player who dealt most damage gets the loot; combat limited by level range that widens deeper; looters follow killers [https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Wilderness]
- Free versus members split of areas, quests and items [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuneScape_Classic]
- RuneScape 2 (3D) beta December 2003, released 29 March 2004; the original was kept online as RuneScape Classic [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuneScape]
- Simple backend that let developers prototype content quickly [https://www.pcgamesn.com/longest-game-making-runescape]

## 5 Known flaws and regrets
- December 2007: Jagex removed free-for-all PvP loot and unbalanced trading to curb real-world trading; subscriptions reportedly fell by 60,000 [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuneScape]. Jagex's stated reason: remote Wilderness corners enabled real-world item trades and PK loot would vanish with the death change; PvP moved to a Bounty Hunter area (about 40% of the old size) [https://runescape.wiki/w/Wilderness_removal]. Sources differ on whether "free PvP" was fully removed; the wiki says it was relocated, not eliminated.
- Wilderness was restored 1 February 2011 after a player referendum [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuneScape]
- Funding plan (ads) failed and had to be replaced by membership [https://www.engadget.com/2010-12-07-the-game-archaeologist-and-the-forbidden-runescape-words-with-p.html]
- Developers later admitted the social roots were forgotten as core gameplay intensified, so players chatted less (Ogilvie, as reported) [https://www.pcgamesn.com/longest-game-making-runescape]
- Eurogamer (2009) described the community as unfriendly to newcomers [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuneScape]
- Jagex later revived a 2007 snapshot as Old School RuneScape after poll interest (449,351 votes, launched 22 February 2013), a sign players valued that earlier design [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_School_RuneScape]

## 6 What Frankendom should take
1. Zero-friction entry: playable instantly in a browser with short sessions in mind. SOURCED (design intent) [https://www.pcgamesn.com/longest-game-making-runescape]
2. Ship small content often rather than big drops; keep a simple content-authoring backend. SOURCED [https://www.pcgamesn.com/longest-game-making-runescape]
3. For many-vs-one and loot-on-death fights, bound risk with level-range limits, a "who dealt most damage gets the loot" rule and a skull-style flag. SOURCED mechanics [https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Wilderness]; application to Frankendom INFERRED.
4. If loot can transfer between players, plan for real-world trading and botting from day one; Jagex had to gut PvP loot in 2007. SOURCED lesson [https://runescape.wiki/w/Wilderness_removal]
5. Keep the progression loop skill-by-use so every action visibly moves a bar. INFERRED from the XP-per-action description [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RuneScape]
