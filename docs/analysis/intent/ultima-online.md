## 1 Design pillars
- Garriott conceived a fantasy game for thousands of players in one shared world; stated goal was to give players as much freedom as possible. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultima_Online]
- A social and economic experiment where players are not the centre of attention as in single-player games. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultima_Online]
- Skill-based progression with no levels or classes; craft-based, player-driven economy; persistent player housing. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultima_Online]
- Players may take villain roles; unrestricted PvP at launch. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultima_Online]
- Player self-governance: design empowering players rather than PvP toggles or heavy admin enforcement, including local laws, town stones and militias. [https://www.raphkoster.com/?p=32112] [https://www.raphkoster.com/?p=16034]
- A virtual ecology was intended; it was cut because players killed creatures faster than they respawned. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultima_Online] [https://www.raphkoster.com/?p=32112]

## 2 Why players loved it
- Next Generation praised the large world and real players; it won Online Game of the Year at the first Interactive Achievement Awards. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultima_Online]
- Internal projection was about 15,000 subscribers at most; it reached 100k by Dec 1998 and 250k by Oct 2000. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultima_Online]
- Emergent play: over a dozen guilds formed in alpha; a player killed Lord British during beta using a fire field (an invulnerability reset was missed). [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultima_Online]
- Players could build economies, houses and governments with real consequences. [https://www.raphkoster.com/?p=16034]

## 3 Pacing and progression feel
- No levels: skills advance with use (Wikipedia: skill-based, no levels or classes). [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultima_Online]
- Early death was harsh: corpses could be looted within seconds and players were killed repeatedly after reviving at distant shrines. [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/a-brief-history-of-murder-in-ultima-online]
- New players were disproportionately hit by trap-based ambushes, which slowed subscriber growth. [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/a-brief-history-of-murder-in-ultima-online]
- Mid and late game feel (house building, crafting, guild wars): partly sourced via systems above; detailed pacing UNSOURCED.
- Age of Shadows (2003) added five resistance types, weapon properties and item insurance (loot-risk softening). [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultima_Online]

## 4 Signature features and systems
- Reputation: first notoriety titles (1997), then hidden karma and fame with colour-coded names (blue innocent, gray recently flagged, red murderer; five murders = red) in spring 1998. [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/a-brief-history-of-murder-in-ultima-online]
- Bounties and Virtue Guards factions, guildstones and guild war declarations. [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/a-brief-history-of-murder-in-ultima-online]
- Trammel/Felucca split (May 2000): Trammel consent-only PvP, Felucca full PvP; Siege servers with harsher rules. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultima_Online]
- Player housing, crafting economy. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultima_Online]

## 5 Known flaws and regrets
- Koster calls the handling of player killing the team's biggest mistake, and a business mistake: Trammel roughly doubled population and sharply cut churn. [https://www.raphkoster.com/?p=32112]
- He now says the support cost of PK switches only became prohibitive at about ten times UO's player count, so the original assumption was wrong. [https://www.raphkoster.com/?p=32112]
- Each fix opened a new exploit (stat decay spacing, bounty gaming, traps used as weapons); many players left for EverQuest's simpler PvP toggle; the Trammel split removed much emergent economic play. [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/a-brief-history-of-murder-in-ultima-online]
- Koster's 1998 position: when combat is removed, harassment moves to other channels (words, stalking), so no single tool fully fixes it. [https://www.raphkoster.com/?p=16034]
- Launch bugs, severe lag at 100k subs, a poorly reviewed 3D client; critic scores were mixed (Metacritic 59). [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultima_Online]

## 6 What Frankendom should take

2. Protect new players from repeat ganking and corpse-camping (fast respawn away from the killer, loot-protection windows). Problem SOURCED [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/a-brief-history-of-murder-in-ultima-online]; specific fixes INFERRED.
3. Use a visible, simple reputation colour (blue/gray/red) so players can read danger before engaging. SOURCED mechanic [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/a-brief-history-of-murder-in-ultima-online]; many-vs-one fairness use is INFERRED.
