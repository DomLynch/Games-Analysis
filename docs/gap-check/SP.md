## Scaling gaps for 1k and 10k players (what the repo has today)
- Tick: presence 100 ms (interest.ts RULES.tickMs, 10 Hz). Ticker polls every 25 ms, no overrun drop (server.ts:46-62). Tick time is recorded, 40,000 samples (server.ts:18, ticks.ts); spec target longest <50 ms, p99 <25 ms (ticks.ts:1). No measured result found in the repo checkout.
- Per-layer limits: soft 80, hard 100 (interest.ts:21-22), 8 layers by default (main.ts, PRESENCE_MAX_LAYERS) = about 640 players joinable by default, 800 at hard cap. So 1k players needs more layers (a flag change) and 10k needs about 125 layers: untested, and a single Node process.
- Interest management: 16 m grid, 40 m radius, 40 nearest, tiers 10/5/2 Hz (interest.ts:9-13). Per tick every viewer scans its cells and sorts: cost grows with crowd density, not total players. Worst crowd 100 in one 40 m radius is capped at 40 drawn.
- Per-connection limits: 8 sockets per IP, 30 joins per minute per IP, 30 messages/s per socket, 64-byte message cap, 30 s idle (server.ts:16). No outbound backpressure (see ADD).
- Duel relay: 2000 rooms, 5000 users, 64 KB/s per room (scripts/duel-relay.mjs:33), separate process.
- Persistence cadence: writer accepts location on leave and every 60 s (origins/server/location.ts:4); I did not find the presence-side sender in origins/presence/ (only ops/presence.env.example mentions the key), so whether it posts every 60 s today is unverified. Position memory: 10 minutes, 10,000 accounts (interest.ts memoryMs/memoryMax).
- Database path: every writer call spawns a psql process (origins/server/db.ts:15-33; handlers.ts:33 says an open costs 1 + pending processes, up to 51). This is the first thing that breaks at 1k concurrent opens; no connection pool.
- Single point of failure: presence, writer, verifier and relay all on one VPS (one-shard.md section 6 table).
- Server-side fights: none; verification replays each record (origins/server/encounter-verify.ts), so CPU cost per settled fight is one replay, not a live tick.
