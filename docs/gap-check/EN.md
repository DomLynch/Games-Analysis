# Gap check EN (encounter templates: ambush, camp, patrol, lair, escort)
Date 2026-10-12. Frankendom repo read: frankendom-lead-catalogue at commit 517b32248 (read-only). Donor notes: 20 (EN001 to EN010, two donors each). This is a game-feel/world topic, so rows are grouped by theme, not one row per note.

## Summary for Dom
1. Frankendom already has the CAMP half of encounter templates: a zone's creature budget, whole camps of 2 to 4 with a leader, an opener camp near the door, a camp dropped into any 15-second dead stretch, and a one-attacker-at-a-time crowd director (origins/mobs/populate.ts, origins/crowd/crowd.ts). A world boss state machine from the same ModernUO idea also exists (origins/boss/boss.ts).
2. Missing: ambush with an arrival rule, patrol loops, a spawn director (respawn grace, per-tick budget, gates), and group assist (fight-noise pull, social pull). The last one is written as a spec (docs/specs/origins/mobs.md sections 2.5, 2.6) but has no code. Counts by theme row: REUSE 7, ADD 5, SKIP 2 (14 rows).
3. Every donor note says "lands in origins/server/world-spawns.ts" or "src/fight/*.ts". Neither exists in this checkout (origins/server/ has no world-spawns.ts; there is no src/fight/). The nearest real homes are origins/mobs/ (rows and populate), origins/world/ (zone rules, schema), origins/crowd/ (attackers) and origins/boss/.
4. Top 3 additions: (a) G1 a zone spawn director (respawn with grace, per-tick creature budget, trigger gates for ambush and lair, all server-rolled from the zone seed); (b) G2 two new template shapes on the mob row, ambush and patrol, as data; (c) G3 build the group assist from mobs.md 2.5/2.6 (a hit pulls the camp, capped, extras queue).
5. Licence note: only 2004Scape, daggerfall-unity and OpenTESArena (all MIT) and Barony (BSD-2) are adaptable; their game content is not. Angband, Crawl, Shattered Pixel Dungeon, EQEmu, ACE (AGPL), AAEmu (conflicting), azerothcore, cmangos, ModernUO, OpenDAoC, openmw are copyleft: shape only. Cataclysm is CC BY-SA 3.0; DevilutionX, Fallout 1 and 2 are non-commercial only: ideas only. No donor numbers are in this file.

## What we have today
- Mob row: weight, roam, spread, pull, campSize, bossCamp (origins/mobs/row.ts:16-31), DEFAULTS (row.ts:49), CAMP_MAX 4 and BOSS_CAMP_MAX 6 (row.ts:53-54), level-band weights for a zone (rungWeights, row.ts:100-102), rarity weights (row.ts:50).
- Generator: populateZone makes camps from rows, a zone's Params and a seed; budget by density; opener camp near the entry; fill camp in any dead stretch; camps at least 6 m apart; deterministic and pure (origins/mobs/populate.ts:1-18, 20-31, 56-75).
- Zone rules as checks: no zone over 45 s across, something every 15 s, first fight within 10 s (origins/world/zone-rules.ts:1-13). Zone data already has `spawns.respawnSeconds` and a `boss` anchor (origins/world/schema.ts:76-78) and a `safe` flag (schema.ts:111).
- Attackers: tag-team crowd, ONE holder fights, the rest circle at 4 to 6 m, up to 8 in a pack, roles duellist/brute/skirmisher/leader (origins/crowd/crowd.ts:15-34, 38). This is stricter than "3 attackers": one at a time.
- Camp look: fire pit, seats, bedrolls as data (origins/preview/frontier-camp.ts:1-5; camp-fire.ts:1-3).
- Boss event: dormant, gathering (kill a wave to fill a bar), boss, defeated, restart cooldown, abandon (origins/boss/boss.ts:5-6, 41, 57); README says spawn population and roster are not done (origins/boss/README.md).
- World fight: fightSetup, fightSeed, resolveFight, flee twist (origins/encounters/README.md; src/twist.ts:4-11); the server replays the fight (origins/server/encounter-verify.ts:1-9).
- Mob styles shy/bold/ambusher on the fight side (origins/mobs/kits.ts:25-33).
- Spec only, no code found by grep in src/ or origins/: fight-noise pull and social pull (docs/specs/origins/mobs.md:224-238), a "?" then "!" alert beat (mobs.md:197), slot-based spawning (mobs.md section 4). Region 1 rules out escorts: "no collect-N steps, no escorts" (docs/specs/origins/region1-ash-frontier.md:223).
- Not present: origins/server/world-spawns.ts, src/fight/, any patrol route, any ambush trigger, per-tick NPC budget.

## Merged additions (one build each)
- G1 Zone spawn director (server). Notes: EQEmu, ACE, AAEmu, DevilutionX, flare, OpenDAoC, ModernUO, 2004Scape, openmw, azerothcore, cmangos, Barony. Rules to carry: respawn delay and variance as zone data (extend schema.ts:78); despawn-on-empty only after a grace timer; a creature budget per server tick with a rotating start so a crowd costs the same; ambush and lair are gates flipped by a server event, never by the client; every roll comes from the zone seed (same stream style as populate.ts:18) so the server can verify; no refill after a boss dies; a slot freed by a death starts the delay. Overlaps origins/boss/boss.ts restart rule: reuse it, do not rewrite.
- G2 Template shapes on the mob row. Notes: Angband, Crawl, SPD, CDDA, flare, azerothcore, cmangos, Barony, Fallout 2, 2004Scape, openmw. Rules: add an optional `shape` (camp default, ambush, patrol) so existing rows are unchanged; members by family tag drawn from the 100 Pit characters, not per-zone stat blocks; ambush arrives out of view at a minimum distance, with a warning beat; patrol is a short route with stop times as data and a facing cone decided on the server; at most 3 in attack slots (crowd.ts holds this), extras wait on the ring.
- G3 Group assist. Notes: flare, azerothcore, openmw, 2004Scape, ModernUO. Rules: pull only camp members within a radius; cap per fight window; extras go to the ring and join the crowd director (mobs.md:233 already says so); alert decays on a timer and drops when the target dies. Build from mobs.md, not from donors.
