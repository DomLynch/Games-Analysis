## What we have today (shared by all rows)
- Entry: welcome card with a name form, "Enter the arena" (index.html:20). The first frame is warmed behind the versus card before it lifts (src/first-frame.ts:1-4, 17-29).

- Once-flags: device-local only (LESSON_DONE_KEY, PACE_KEY; src/lessons.ts:26, 33); the fatigue line shows once (src/main.ts:1920-1922).
- Feedback: hit-stop and camera knock on every landed hit, always on (src/hit-impact.ts:1-8); damage numbers exist, off by default, white dealt and red taken (src/hud.ts:35, src/main.ts:774).
- First reward: take-one kill screen, a tap is the take, Undo (src/loot-panel.ts:1-12); career level = 1 + wins, so the first win is Recruit II (src/career.ts:3-7, SCOPE.md:48); death gets at most two plain lines, the warden's action then the player's habit (src/autopsy.ts:1-5).
- Hub: Pit gate opens over 5 s (src/pit/gate.ts:1-3); crowd cue 10 s after arrival (src/pit/pit.ts:12-14); the Concord Exchange has covenant stone, contract board, forge and bank as landmarks, and is a safe zone (origins/world/concord.ts:15, 33-37). A fresh join spawns at the Pit yard centre (docs/specs/origins/launch-gates.md, X2). The outer gate opens at Gladiator (docs/specs/origins/region1-ash-frontier.md:4; living-world.md:499).
- Quests and talk engines (built, content thin): journal (origins/quests/journal.ts:1-30), talk with conditions and once-lines (origins/talk/talk.ts:1-20), first giver Marrow at the contract board (origins/region1/content.ts:119, 188).
- Pull-forward today: rank row with next rank (src/main.ts:130-132), `next` opponent in the HUD view (src/hud.ts:18), Pit record board and skull wall (src/pit/board.ts:1-4), per-opponent legends (docs/SCOPE.md legends line).

## Merged additions (one build each)
- G1 Hint ladder (crawl, Mindustry, Cataclysm, endless-sky, keeperfx, azerothcore and cmangos hint bits, Evan reveal test). One hint on screen at a time, in the existing status line, 25 words or fewer at 375 wide. Each hint has a show-when state and a done-when SERVER event (first take, first rank-up, first Exchange entry, second attacker), a Skip, and a once flag held on the account (the one ledger), not the device. Rules to carry: a calm gate (no hint while a foe is swinging); never during the first loss; the control the hint names flashes until tapped. FIX with it: write the first-loss done flag when the lesson ENDS, not when it starts (src/main.ts:1283). Reuse the lessons.ts shape, extend it.
- G2 Next-goal line (Barony, plus keeperfx rival line). After a win the kill screen says one goal: "N more in this pass", the next opponent by name (hud.ts:18 already carries it) and a one-line rival or legend tease (legends.ts exists). No new screen. Look test: kill screen with and without the line.
- G3 Exchange service tour (flare, daggerfall, 2004Scape, keeperfx, endless-sky, DOLSharp, ModernUO). On first entry to the Exchange, services unlock one at a time as a three-to-five step journal chain: contract board (Marrow), bank, forge, covenant stone. Each stop flashes until visited; a locked door shows a one-line reason; the first payout is one named line in the ledger, no fanfare. Gates are counted steps from server events, never a clock. The state table is data in zone/quest rows, run by origins/quests/journal.ts and origins/talk/talk.ts. The clan choice at the outer gate (living-world.md:510) is the natural step 4.
- G4 Zone arrival and first-visit reward (angband, cmangos, Devilution presence idea). A new zone shows one threat line on entry and one loot line after a set amount explored; first visit pays a once-only discovery row in the award model; an NPC can be present only while its quest is open. All fields are zone data, thresholds ours.
