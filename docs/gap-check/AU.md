5. Licence note: GPL/AGPL/CC-BY-SA donors (0 A.D. art, Cataclysm-DDA, Brogue, Flare game, KeeperRL, Hedgewars, Teeworlds, OpenTTD, many engines) are ideas only for us unless Dom decides share-alike is acceptable; the earlier research (docs/research/legends-600-bodies-and-donors.md:153) already marks GPL art RED and CC-BY-SA AMBER. ## Top 10 additions (G1-G10) with rules to carry
- G1 Music and ambience layer. One bed per place and time of day, music fades on zone change, quiet during combat hits (combat bus stays master); original or CC0/own-synth only. Never a donor track. Check Return-To-The-Roots CC0 tracks file by file before any use, credit if CC-BY.
- G2 Origins biome ground set: ash waste, rock, marsh, water, lava, cave. PBR triples (colour, normal, roughness) at 512, like `public/pit/stone/`; animated flow only as a shader, not a video. Recreated, not copied, from 117HD/0 A.D. layouts.
- G3 Settlement and ruin prop kit: wall pieces, fences, cart, brazier, signpost, 12 to 20 pieces, shared material atlas. Our own geometry; Quaternius CC0 as base if needed.
- G4 Bodies for the three missing families (giant, quadruped, small drake): rows over a shared rig, element variants by tint/trail only. Follows `body-families.md` plan.
- G5 Weather and ambient particles: ash fall, dust storm, rain, snow, embers. Pooled points like `src/foot-dust.ts:22-30`, one preset per zone `weather` key.
- G6 Item and status icon family: slot, material, element; three-tone key by rank (same as `rank-01..10`). Drawn originally.
- G7 Ground loot container and drop pop (sack, chest open) tied to take-one loot rule; server-verified kills only.
- G8 One asset licence manifest and credits page: file path, licence, author, source URL, sha256, "modified" flag; test fails on a new asset without a row.
- G9 Hit-number and floating text style (Flare, Tibia, MapleStory ideas): coloured by damage type, pooled, mobile-readable. Check combat HUD owners before building.
- G10 Per-creature voice kits: finish `src/audio/creature.ts` sets per family, Dom picks by hand.
