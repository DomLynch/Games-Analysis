## Summary for Dom
1. Frankendom has two cameras. The fight camera (src/camera.ts) is mature: orbit, duel lock, hit kick, roll tilt, finisher reveals, reduced-motion handling. The world camera is about 10 lines inline in the greybox walker (origins/preview/main.ts:259-266): fixed 5.2 m behind, 2.7 m up, eased follow, look 3 m ahead, a saved "camera locked" chip that turns the hero toward the nearest creature within 9 m. It has NO shoulder offset, NO wall pull-in, NO fade, NO group framing, NO presets and no `?cam=` flag. The five presets A to E are designed only (nothing built).
2. Counts by theme row (13 rows): REUSE 3, ADD 7, SKIP 3 (several rows are mixed; see table). By donor note: about 38 notes feed ADD themes, 12 feed REUSE, 19 are SKIP (2D tile cameras, first-person, server-scripted cameras, or no camera code).
3. Top 3 additions: (a) G1 shoulder rig with wall pull-in as one new pure camera module (OpenMW shape for the shoulder, a fast-in slow-out pull-in, sphere sweep, lift, near-wall hero fade); (b) G2 a fight-framing part for the world: frame the hero plus all engaged foes along the screen-vertical axis with a hard distance cap, and lock-on that picks by thumb direction; (c) G3 the `?cam=A|B` preview flag (look-test pattern) with a test that the fight/Pit camera output is unchanged.
4. Two rulings block the build. "Camera: frozen" (origins/world/README.md:59-61, docs/specs/origins/region1-ash-frontier.md:11, docs/state/character.md:98: no camera changes or A/B tests unless Dom asks) and the v3 plan to put a `camera` block in zone rows, which the same README says world data cannot change. Both need Dom's word before the World lane starts. His six open questions (docs/patterns/camera-OPEN-QUESTIONS-for-dom.md) are still unanswered; v3 says he answers by playing A and B.
5. Licence note: many camera donors are copyleft or restricted (GPL, LGPL, AGPL, Valve SDK, Sustainable Use, CC-BY-SA, WoWee "no commercial game", no-licence repos). For those this file gives shape only and no donor numbers. MIT/Apache/BSD donors may be named. The earlier brainstorm check found preset A and D copied WoWee and roBrowser numbers; v3 replaced them, and any new constant must be tuned on the phone, not taken from a donor.

## What we have today
- Fight camera, pure poses: src/camera.ts:26-82 `cameraPose` (orbit 7.5 m back; duel lock back max(4.2, d*0.62+2.8), height max(3.2, d*1.3), look at the midpoint). Rig with state: camera.ts:181-418 `createCameraRig` (exp ease `1 - exp(-dt*8)` at :264 and :389, so frame-rate independent).
- Short and low foes: a one-time over-the-left-shoulder step plus lift so a short foe is not hidden (camera.ts:19-23, 46-50, 54-60). This is NOT a user shoulder offset; it is a framing fix and only in the lock.
- Orbit and recentre: camera.ts:219-226 (yaw 0.005/px, pitch clamp 0.22 to 0.9 rad, recenter). Lock yaw follows the foe line (:265-267).
- Bounds: hard radius clamp 11.5 m (camera.ts:49-53; src/arena.ts:21 CAMERA_CLAMP); arena rule that nothing solid stands inside it (src/arena.ts:16, src/arena-props.ts:12). So the fight camera has no wall problem by construction.
- Hit feel: src/camera-kick.ts:8-17 `shoveFor` (a few cm, settles 0.12 to 0.22 s), camera.ts:251 `shove`, roll tumble `tilt` (camera.ts:261, src/hit-impact.ts:26). Reduced motion keeps the kick on by ruling (camera.ts:151-153) and drops the finisher push-in (:154-155). Closed research: distance-scaled kick "not needed" (docs/state/world.md:5-7).
- Spec lines that constrain us: GAME_SPEC.md:147 (follow: no head bob or shake), :148 (lock: no manual camera while locked), :236 (no cuts, no FOV punch, no lens overlay). Page pinch zoom is blocked (src/main.ts:78-79).
- World camera: origins/preview/main.ts:58 (fov 51, near 0.1, far 180), :259-266 (follow: back 5.2 / up 2.7, in passages 3.4 / 2.1; eased with `1 - exp(-dt*4)`; look 3 m ahead at 1.5 m; the right stick tilts via `pitchNow` damped at :238). Free look by a one-finger drag turns the hero heading (:427, 0.006 per px, not aspect-normalised). Lock-on: `mobs.nearest(x, z, 9)` with a saved toggle (:219-220, :228-229; origins/preview/mobs-view.ts:55). The hero is stopped by `canStand` (:89, :243), but the camera eye is never tested against walls.
- Frozen constants: origins/world/schema.ts:28-35 `CAMERA` (fov 51, follow back 5.2, height 2.7, ahead 3, settle 4 per second; passage 3.4 / 2.1), doc origins/world/README.md:59-61.
- Flag pattern to copy: src/look-flag.ts:1-12 (`?look=` parser) and `?look=foe` in camera.ts:9, :382.
- Tests that exist: tests/camera.test.ts, tests/camera-kick.test.ts. Nothing for a world camera, shoulder, pull-in, framing or presets. No `src/fight/` folder exists (v3 says World is "extracting" one; not present at this commit). MAX_ATTACKERS / an engaged set is not in src or origins code; only the idea in docs/specs/origins/combat-study.md:218.

## Merged additions (one build each)
- G1 World shoulder rig with wall pull-in. Notes: OpenMW, SRB2, L2J, dhewm3, ioq3, OpenLara, OpenEQ, OpenAC, WoWee, benilla, fallout1/2 (shape), Open-RSC (turn-away shape). One pure module (no browser calls, offsets as plain data; client-only, never read by the server or the sim). Rules to carry: shoulder is a fraction of the visible half-width at that distance so the hero stays in frame at 375x812 (v3 uses 0.30 m at 3.2 m, from the check P1 table, not 0.55); switch sides with hysteresis (one enter and one leave distance) so corridors do not flip; pull-in is fast and give-back is slow, started from the clamped eye; sphere sweep from the focus point not the hero's feet; focus height about 0.68 of hero height (feet and thumb zone); camera yaw stays within 90 degrees of directly behind; never hide the hero (fade the body when pulled below a minimum). Time constants in seconds, tuned on the phone (v3 starting values, our own). Test first: a headless case that the eye never ends inside a wall.
- G2 Fight framing for the world camera. Notes: SpacetimeDB, CnC, Ikemen, runelite, OpenAC. Rules: frame the engaged foes only (the engine's attacker slots, waiting foes ignored); fit along the screen-vertical axis first; hard maximum distance equal to the zone band max; targets behind the near plane ignored; lock-on picks by thumb direction and the camera never feeds picking; kick table reused unchanged. Depends on an engaged-set export from combat (not present today).
- G3 Look input and preview switch. Notes: halflife, vthibault, esponges, unityro, OpenTESArena, Microverse, ClassicUO. Rules: a look goal separate from hero heading; clamps at the write; look normalised by the smaller screen side; return to behind-the-hero while moving; one restore control. Ship as a `?cam=A|B` flag copied from src/look-flag.ts (look-test skill), with a test that `?cam=` and all post effects leave the fight and Pit camera output byte-identical (check P9). Needs Dom's answers to questions 1 and 4 before tuning.
- G4 Per-zone limits row. Notes: Card-Forge, lincity, amilich, midgard, unityro. Only after Dom lifts the freeze: a small `camera.limits` group in origins/world/schema.ts read at zone load; the server uses it only for the spawn-view proxy, never the live camera.
- Deferred experiments (keep, do not build yet): C Runner (speed pull-back, look-ahead), D Cinematic Lag (spring, leash box, occluder fade), E Tight Pocket (corridors).
