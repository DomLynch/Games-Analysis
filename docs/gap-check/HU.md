## Summary for Dom
1. The duel HUD is built and works: three meters (opponent, you, stamina) plus two posture bars, seven action buttons that light, dim and shake when a press is refused, floating damage numbers (on by default, greyed), a layout tier word and safe-area padding. What is missing is everything Origins needs: no minimap or zone map, no enemy bar/target frame in the open world, no hit-direction cue, no health-bar polish (lag, last-hit trail).
2. Counts over 79 notes: REUSE 11, ADD 45, SKIP 23. The 45 ADDs collapse to five builds (G1 to G5 below). G5 is parked: it needs fights with more than one enemy at once, which is only an option in docs/specs/origins/combat-study.md:219 (2B), not built. The "up to 3 attackers" in many notes is the donor note's assumption, not a repo constant (no MAX_ATTACKERS in src, origins or docs/specs).
3. Top 3 additions: (a) G4 one zone map (cached base, changed-cells repaint, server-filtered reveal, tap to centre, hit pings); (b) G2 Origins enemy plate (bar shown only when targeted, hurt or threatening, hold-then-hide, range band, red when it targets you; the rule is already written in mobs.md:129 and not built); (c) G1 bar layers (lagging ghost, last-hit trail, tick marks, low-health pulse), pure client view, no new state.
4. Already done and reused: touch ownership (src/touch-router.ts), thumb sticks (origins/preview/sticks.ts), input buffering (src/duel.ts:156), refused-press cue (src/hud.ts:156), con colour and tap card (origins/mobs/info.ts, origins/preview/creature-card.ts), the tier word (src/layout-tier.ts, written to the page but unused by CSS).

Restricted or unclear: OpenBW, Fallout 1/2 CE, DevilutionX, Half-Life SDK, DOOM, Wolfenstein 3D, jynew. ## Merged additions (one build each)
- G1 Bar layers (7 notes). One pure client view: displayed value chases the true value on elapsed time (frame rate must not matter), a hold-then-drain ghost, a coloured strip for the change since the last exchange (regen moves the snapshot so it never reads as loss), tick marks per fixed hp unit as data, low-health edge pulse with an exit band. Rules to carry: the bar never changes health (one ledger); kills stay server-verified; a living enemy never draws below 1 px; reuse the stamina low pulse at hud.ts:86 instead of a second one; ship behind a ?look flag with phone stills (visual-pr-stills). Lands in src/hud.ts and src/style.css.
- G2 Origins enemy plate (8 notes, incl. Exult's 200 ms hit flash that always restarts on the latest hit). Shown when targeted, hurt (hold restarts, never stacks), or hunting the hero; hidden at full health; wider hide range than show range (hysteresis band); unseen target draws a dash box, not a stale number; neutral con colour, red when it targets you (mobs.md:129); status words separate from health colour. Pure rule in origins/mobs/info.ts beside conBand, drawing in mobs-view.ts. Owner per mobs.md: Expansion for rules, Characters for art.
- G3 Lock-on rules (8 notes). Pick order: in range, in cone, nearest, tie-break by id, sight test last; hold the lock for a short grace after the last hit (window refreshed on each hit); selected and engaged are two states (first tap selects, second engages); reach and cone are ability data. The client proposes, the server re-checks and decides the hit. Lands in origins/preview/main.ts and mobs-view.ts.
- G4 Zone map (18 notes). Zone size and bounds from zone data; base layer cached and repainted only for changed cells in a per-update slice; one cell-priority chain (mine, mobs, hostile pings, terrain, unexplored); fog is the server's reveal only, never client knowledge of hidden positions; tap centres the view, clamped to the zone; hit ping shrinks for a fixed life with a per-source cooldown; one zoom clamp shared by corner map and full map; a drag below a pixel tolerance is a tap; touch targets stay 44 px. New file (none exists) plus a per-player filter next to origins/presence/interest.ts.
- G5 Multi-enemy readouts, parked (4 notes). Pain cue per side, off-screen arrows fanned apart with a cap and a derived count, one summed group bar. Do not start before 2B multi-fighter duels (combat-study.md:219) exist.
