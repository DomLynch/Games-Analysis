Grep for front_index and back_index finds only the parser, the writer and a test that checks values. So the rendering mechanism the cell asks about is not in this donor, only its data layout. Best transferable idea: Store a per-zone far-plane distance and fog range as zone data (values a designer edits per volume), so the draw distance is authored per zone instead of one global constant.

Decision (proposed): adapt - zone far plane and fog as zone data fit Frankendom's zones-as-data rule and give a real cull distance and fade; the BSP leaf, portal and occluder machinery does not fit, because the donor has no runtime to copy and Frankendom zones are not BSP-built.
