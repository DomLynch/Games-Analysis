## Inspiration (category-level for effects; own words; not read deeply)
- Effect catalog as one row per skill: a row chooses asset-backed effect files or a named procedural handler, with a fallback in the same row. Frankendom: a spell row that points at our own VFX asset or a procedural effect, with a fallback. Read: header and row shape (RagEffectCatalog.generated.inc lines 1 to 14).
- Named procedural effect handlers: an enum lists portal, ready-portal, warp-zone (two generations), map pillar, map magic zone, map particle, recovery and others (src/world/RagEffect.h around line 175). Idea: zone gates and map landmarks as named procedural effects, not assets.
- Layered effects from bands and segments: EffectBandState (RagEffect.h line 44), EffectSegmentState (line 58), and CEffectPrim (line 75) show that an effect is built from parts with their own state. Idea: a magic effect with a ground ring, a column and sparks as separate timed parts. Only the class layout was read.
- Effects that outlive or detach from their owner: CRagEffect exposes DetachFromMaster and OnActorDeleted (RagEffect.cpp lines 4259 and 4294; names only). Idea: when a caster dies the aura is left behind and fades, which can carry the death presentation (Frankendom row 17).
- Effect-linked sound: TryPlaySingleEffectWave (RagEffect.cpp line 1642) and PlayEzStrAssociatedStartupSound (line 1676) play a sound chosen by the effect's asset name (names only). Idea: one signature sound tied to a spell row.
- Gate effects: SpawnPortal (line 4432) and the warp-zone and ready-portal spawners in the same file (names only). Idea: a portal reads as a column of particles plus a ground ring.

## Briefs
label: Left-behind aura
why: when a caster drops, the aura hangs in the world and fades, so the loss reads without a message.
keep: the effect detaches from its owner when the owner is removed and runs its own timeline; it does not vanish with the owner.
change: an ember ring that drifts up instead of a column; colour from the zone palette, not a fixed tint; shorter fade so it fits a fast duel.
ref: Sziadan-open-midgard/src/world/RagEffect.cpp (DetachFromMaster line 4259, OnActorDeleted line 4294)
Rule: recreate as a clearly distinct original, never a copy. Kept: detach-and-fade. Changed: shape, colour source, timing.

label: Gate pillar
why: a portal that reads as a vertical column plus a ground ring can be seen from far away and at 375 px wide.
keep: a vertical particle column with a ground ring marks the gate; the ring is a separate part.
change: a split rift shape in two tones instead of a plain column; the ring carries a zone name plate.
ref: Sziadan-open-midgard/src/world/RagEffect.cpp (SpawnPortal line 4432); enum in src/world/RagEffect.h line 175
Rule: recreate as a clearly distinct original, never a copy. Kept: column plus ring. Changed: rift shape, tones, name plate.

label: Spell row with fallback
why: a single data row that names an asset effect and a procedural fallback lets content ship before art does.
keep: row holds an asset effect and a procedural handler name; the handler is used when the asset is missing.
change: our row also holds a threat tint and a sound id; our handler names are our own words.
ref: Sziadan-open-midgard/src/world/RagEffectCatalog.generated.inc (header lines 1 to 4)
Rule: recreate as a clearly distinct original, never a copy.

label: Banded spell effect
why: an effect built from a few timed bands reads as one motion and is cheap to tune.
keep: an effect is a short list of bands, each with segments and its own state.
change: fewer, larger shapes so it reads at phone size; one band is always the impact flash.
ref: Sziadan-open-midgard/src/world/RagEffect.h (EffectBandState line 44, EffectSegmentState line 58, CEffectPrim line 75)
Rule: recreate as a clearly distinct original, never a copy.
