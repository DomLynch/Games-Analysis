Why PARTIAL: the donor has a real server-side camera cue outbox, but the motion itself (how rate, rate2 and height move the camera, how look-at tracks, how shake is applied) is client code that is not in this repo, so the mechanism that matters for a camera is not in the donor. The file's own comment says the queue is sent "after rebuild", but updateMap runs for every connected player in each client-output pass (World.ts 1108), so the flush is a per-pass outbox, not a replay on rebuild.

What the donor does, as read: CAM_LOOKAT and CAM_MOVETO are queued per player as CameraInfo (types 1 and 0, with a speed and a second rate), sent once by updateMap as CamLookAt or CamMoveTo, and then unlinked; CAM_SHAKE and CAM_RESET are written at once and are never queued; a player cleanup clears the queue (Player.ts 459). The client reports pitch and yaw back as two-byte values into input tracking (InputTracking.ts 58).

Best transferable idea: Keep server camera cues as data with a speed per move and send shake and reset immediately, but only once the client-side motion they drive is read, since that motion is what a camera needs.
Decision (proposed): reject - Frankendom's ruling keeps the camera client-only and the engine never reads it, and a server-sent cutscene cue has no place in the fight engine; if a zone needs an intro shot it can be zone data read by the client, which this donor does not show.
Not read: the client camera code (not in this repo), how often processClientsOut runs, the body of CamLookAt and CamMoveTo models.
