Why PARTIAL: the kill record is real and read, but it is a summary of one death (killer, weapon, damage, victim HP, survivor roll), not a replay, spectator view, coach channel or wager; the donor's own note says the killmail display was never built.

What is real: at death, ShipSE::Killed builds one KillData record (473-493): the victim, the killer and final blow (killer id 481, weapon, damage total 488), the victim's hull+armour+shield total (490-493), then a survivor roll per item (538, even result survives; 541 splits the quantity with a random int), then a time stamp (554) and LogKill (557).
Defects: the security status field is a placeholder (487, "fix this"); the item-survival comment says 50% but the code is an even-check on a random int whose range was not read; the record is written inside the death routine that also handles pods, insurance and wrecks, so it cannot be reused alone.

Best transferable idea: a fight card written once at the moment of death (killer, weapon, damage total, victim capacity, time), saved as one record that a lobby, a kill card or a replay index can read.
Decision (proposed): adapt — take the one-record-per-death shape as the fight record for row 19; reject the random per-item survival roll (it is a loot rule and conflicts with take-one loot).
Acceptance: a server test asserts that one fight-card record is written per death with killer, weapon, damage total and victim capacity filled, and that no item is removed by the record step.
