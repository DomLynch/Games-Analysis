## 5. Inspiration (category level, our own words)
- Vehicle breakdown is a hazard with memory: every roll that misses adds 1 to a hidden "chance" value and sometimes adds 25 more (vehicle.cpp:1350-1353). A hit resets it. That is a pity counter that makes streaks of misses end. Use it for parry/crit/dodge in fast duels.
- A failure is telegraphed by a state before it bites: state 2 = "going to break down now", then state 1 = broken, and a countdown state for the rest (vehicle.cpp:1378-1388). Readable warning before a hard effect fits a 375 px fight camera.
- A quality stat is turned into a lookup table: reliability >> 10 indexes a 64-entry table of chances (vehicle.cpp:1305, 1363). Cheap to tune, and a designer can read the curve.
- Reliability drains on every check, not only when moving (vehicle.cpp:1331-1333), with a rest exemption for one state (loading, vehicle.cpp:1328-1329). The breakdown roll itself needs speed of at least 5 and a vehicle not stopped (vehicle.cpp:1342-1344). The exemption is the design idea: rest at a safe spot.
- A reset point gives a grace period: leaving the depot refills reliability and cuts the breakdown chance to a quarter (vehicle.cpp:196-200, comment at line 199). Good for a respawn at a shrine.
- Gear is time-gated by data: each vehicle row carries an introduction date and a window of years (engines.h:26 macro, row example engines.h:103). Our version: gear unlocked by zone or progression stage, not calendar.
- Strings are a flat keyed table of about 4800 entries with prefixes per system (english.txt). Good model for a HUD and news-log string set, separate from logic.
- News and message history is a stack of messages with a history menu (english.txt:534-536 keys; src/news_gui.cpp exists but was not read). Possible hud-ui idea for a fight log.
