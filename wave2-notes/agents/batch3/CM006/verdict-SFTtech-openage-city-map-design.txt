Checked: the engine's pathfinding is hierarchical flow-field routing: a Grid of fixed-size Sectors (grid.h 19, 62), each Sector holding a cost field plus Portals on its borders (sector.h 21; portal.h 27), and FlowFields built from integration fields (flow_field.h 26). Confidence high that this is routing, not layout; medium on the routing detail.

Why PARTIAL, one honest sentence: the only real mechanism is routing through a sector grid, which is relevant to moving through a town but does not decide where roads, hubs, landmarks or signs go, so it is not a city-map design mechanism.

Best transferable idea: split a town into fixed sectors with portal cells on the borders, so that a unit's route is looked up per sector rather than by one global search over the whole zone.

Decision (proposed): reject for now - Frankendom's fights are one-on-one or at most three attackers (MAX_ATTACKERS = 3) in zones of a few screens, so a sector-and-flow-field router pays off only for crowds far larger than our rulings allow; revisit if zone size or crowd size grows.
