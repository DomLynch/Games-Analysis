What the donor actually does (order of operations, as read):

- Actions are sorted by weight, highest first (Hook_AttackStart.cpp:23; SCARActionData.cpp:299).
- For each action, in order: the clip must be inside its time window (SCARActionData.cpp:191); the action type must match the attack type (197); then one gate: roll chance >= random 0-100, and the target lies within distance band plus weapon reach and the yaw arc (212). - A cooldown from fCombatAttackAnimationDrivenDelayTime (default 1.5 in the call) applies only while the attacker is pathing (Hook_AttackStart.cpp:14).
- Combo continuation: an empty payload always continues; otherwise the payload number is a percentage roll (Hook_AttackCombo.cpp:9-12).
- Range math: the max distance is padded by the target's bound radius (Function.cpp:61); the min distance is padded by both bound radii when it is above zero (Function.cpp:62-63, read, not receipted).
